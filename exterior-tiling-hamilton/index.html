Why Consider Hamilton Tiling Service When You’re Exterior Tiling?

If you are installing tiles, don’t forget to consider adding outdoor lighting as well to create a complete exterior look for your home. There are several reasons why this can be beneficial: it can give elegance and sophistication to your property, make it visible during nighttime hours, and help prevent crime by making would-be criminals think twice about approaching your house at night.

To get the most from your outdoor lighting, be intentional about choosing the right kind. For example, if you’re installing decorative tiles in a garden or on a patio, opt for low-wattage lights that won’t disrupt the natural light of day.

If you want to install tile in an enclosed space, such as a hallway or stairwell, consider using high-wattage lights to provide more uniform illumination throughout the area. Also, don’t forget to add motion sensors so that the lights turn on automatically when it gets dark outside!

How to Prepare the Area for the Exterior Tiling Service

When you’re ready to start your exterior tiling project, the first step is to prepare the area where the tiles will be installed. This involves cleaning and repairing any damage to the surface, as well as making sure that the area is level.

If there are any cracks or holes in the surface, they should be filled in with a patching compound. The surface should then be sanded smooth so the tiles have a level surface to adhere to.

If you’re installing tile on a concrete surface, you’ll need to etch the surface with muriatic acid first. This will help the tile adhesive to bond properly to the concrete.

Once the surface is prepared, you can begin installing the tiles. Start by spreading a layer of tile adhesive on the surface with a notched trowel. Then, place the tiles in the adhesive and press them firmly into place.

After the tiles have been installed, allow the adhesive to cure for 24 hours before grouting. Once the grout has been applied, allow it to dry for 24 hours before using the area.
